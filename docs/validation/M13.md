TCP/443 and TCP/22 represent separate Gitea entries; UDP/8002 and original NAT TCP/8000–8001 have explicit separate cases. It retains dual-stack pairing, independent absolute deadlines, active deny precedence, all Binding IDs and typed Grant/Deny provenance. Inactive desired inventory never proves installation.
- nftables batch receipts retain actually emitted and omitted membership. Structural preparation precedes fresh dispatch-time membership calculation, including submillisecond omission. Readback checks table/chain/rule/set/membership structure, legitimate native expiry and bounded timeout metadata; private receipt proofs reject caller mutation. Known NAT admission and translation share one transaction. Failed preparation discards its private queued buffer.
- A serialized coordinator validates complete replacements, rechecks topology, confirms apply before cleanup, retains cumulative historical emitted ownership and advances confirmed policy before reporting cleanup failure. Rejected candidates do not advance state; ambiguous apply/confirmation faults the fixture and blocks cleanup. Explicit empty ownership has exact table removal and distinct strict cleanup, including validated Agent removal and retained retries.
- A cancellable test-only driver uses the latest accepted plan at every wake. Absolute deadlines survive replay; no timer captures an old policy. This is not a production scheduling/recovery service.
